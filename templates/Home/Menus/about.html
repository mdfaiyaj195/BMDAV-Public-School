OUR JOURNEY

School History

B.M. DAV Public School was established to provide students with a strong foundation for lifelong learning. The school continues to bring learners, families and educators together in a supportive community focused on knowledge, character and growth.

OUR PURPOSE

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To nurture confident, compassionate and capable individuals who use their knowledge and values to contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide a balanced and supportive education that encourages academic growth, creativity, discipline, respect and responsibility.

LEADERSHIP

Chairman's Message

Education helps young people discover their abilities and prepare for the future. We are committed to providing an encouraging environment where every student can learn, grow in character and work towards their potential. We value the partnership between students, families and teachers in building a strong school community.

FROM THE PRINCIPAL

Principal's Message

At B.M. DAV Public School, we aim to make learning engaging, inclusive and meaningful. Our teachers encourage students to stay curious, work with dedication and treat others with kindness and respect. Together, we strive to help each learner become confident, responsible and ready for the opportunities ahead.

GOVERNANCE

School Management

The school is guided by its management in partnership with the principal, teachers and staff. Their shared work supports the school's academic and administrative functions, promotes a safe and positive learning environment, and helps the school community move towards its educational goals.

HOW WE LEARN

Educational Philosophy

We believe education should develop the whole learner. Alongside strong academic foundations, students need opportunities to think independently, ask questions, express creativity and practise empathy, integrity and cooperation. We encourage every student to take an active role in learning and to use their abilities responsibly.
