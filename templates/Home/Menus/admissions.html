HOW TO APPLY

Admission Process

  1. Make an enquiryContact the school to learn about availability and the current admission schedule.
  2. Collect the application formRequest the current admission form and instructions from the school office.
  3. Submit the applicationComplete the form and provide the documents requested by the school.
  4. Follow the school's guidanceThe school will inform families about any further interaction, review or next steps.
FAIR AND TRANSPARENT

Admission Policy

Admissions are considered in accordance with the school's current policy, available places and applicable education requirements. Families should provide complete and accurate information with their application and follow the instructions and timelines communicated by the school.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility depends on the class applied for and the student's age, previous education and required records. Please contact the school office for the current class-wise criteria and document checklist before submitting an application.
