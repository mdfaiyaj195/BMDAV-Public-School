OUR BELIEF

Academic Philosophy

We believe learning should build strong foundations while encouraging curiosity, independent thinking and a love of discovery. Students are supported to understand concepts, apply knowledge and take responsibility for their academic growth.

HOW WE TEACH

Curriculum and Teaching Methodology

The curriculum is delivered through structured lessons and age-appropriate learning activities. Teachers use explanation, discussion, practice, projects and regular feedback to help students engage with topics, strengthen understanding and make connections across subjects.

LEARNING GROUPS

Classes and Sections

Students are organised into classes and sections to support focused teaching, classroom participation and individual progress. Each class follows its prescribed academic programme, with teachers guiding students through the learning goals for their level.

YEAR AT A GLANCE

Academic Calendar

The academic calendar provides an overview of the school year, including teaching periods, assessments, holidays and key school activities. Students and families should refer to the latest calendar shared by the school for current dates and schedule updates.

ASSESSMENT

Examination System

Student progress is assessed through a combination of classwork, assignments, projects and scheduled examinations. Regular assessment helps teachers identify learning needs and gives students feedback to strengthen their understanding and preparation.

PROGRESS AND PRIDE

Results and Achievements

We value students' academic progress as well as their effort, perseverance and participation. The school recognises achievements across learning and school activities, and encourages every student to set goals and continue developing their abilities.

STUDY RESOURCES

Books and Syllabus

Each class follows a prescribed syllabus supported by subject textbooks and learning resources. Students should use the book list and syllabus provided by the school for their class and academic year to guide their studies.

FOR STUDENTS AND FAMILIES

Academic Information

Learning Expectations

Attend classes regularly, participate in learning activities, complete assigned work and ask teachers for guidance when needed.

Family Partnership

Families can support learning by staying informed about school communication, encouraging regular study habits and discussing student progress with teachers.
