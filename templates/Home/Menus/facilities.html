A SUPPORTIVE ENVIRONMENT

School Campus

From everyday classrooms to spaces for practical discovery and recreation, our campus supports a balanced school experience. We work to maintain an environment where students feel welcomed, supported and ready to participate.

EVERYDAY LEARNING

Classrooms

Classrooms provide a welcoming setting for lessons, discussion and collaborative activities. Students are encouraged to participate, ask questions and work together as they build knowledge and confidence.

LEARNING BY DISCOVERY

Science Laboratories

Science laboratory learning helps students connect classroom concepts with observation and practical activity. Students use these learning spaces under teacher guidance and follow the instructions provided for safe participation.

DIGITAL LEARNING

Computer Laboratory

The computer laboratory supports students in developing digital awareness and practical technology skills. Activities are guided by teachers and are intended to encourage responsible, purposeful use of technology.

READING AND RESEARCH

Library

The library encourages reading, independent learning and exploration of new ideas. Students can make use of the library as a place to build knowledge, develop study habits and enjoy books.

ACTIVE AND HEALTHY

Sports and Playground

Sports and outdoor play help students develop fitness, coordination, teamwork and sportsmanship. Participation in physical activities supports a healthy balance between learning and recreation.

GETTING TO SCHOOL

Transport Facilities

Transport availability, routes, stops and timings may vary. Families should contact the school office to confirm current transport options and receive the latest guidance before making arrangements.

A CARING ENVIRONMENT

Safety and Security

The school promotes a safe and respectful environment through clear expectations, responsible supervision and cooperation among students, staff and families. Students are encouraged to follow school safety instructions and seek help from staff when needed.

WELLBEING

Health and Hygiene

Good health and hygiene habits are an important part of student wellbeing. The school encourages cleanliness, healthy routines and consideration for the wellbeing of others throughout the school day.
